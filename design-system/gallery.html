01 · Color

One primary, earned accents, a room for red.

Sand is the canvas, ink is the voice, marigold is the single primary — capped at ≤5% of any public viewport. Sage is earned by true "safe" states; amber asks for attention without alarm; critical red exists only inside the caseworker dashboard, never on victim-facing screens.

Fraunces set straight. Inter, quiet. Plex Mono for instruments.

03 · Actions

Buttons that say exactly what they do.

8px corners, 44px touch targets, marigold only for the primary path. Danger exists exclusively in the caseworker app. Loading keeps its label and a slow, calm spinner.

04 · Status language

Two dialects of the same truth.

Caseworkers read Stable / Monitoring / Elevated / Critical beside an AI-score disclosure. Victim-facing screens say Supported / In progress / Needs support soon — never red, never "critical." Status is never conveyed by color alone: every badge carries an icon or dot and a word.

08 · Motion

Gentle, unhurried, reassuring.

Nothing bounces, nothing springs. Entrances fade and rise 8–12px over 360–560ms on a decelerating curve. Every animation defers to prefers-reduced-motion.

09 · The three experiences

One system, three registers.

Enforced by density, not by new tokens: serif-led and generous on the public site, narrow and gentle in the portal, sans-led and compact in the dashboard.
